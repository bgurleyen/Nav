Privacy policy

Zflite is a flight-procedure trainer. This page says what the app stores, why, and how to ask us to delete it. Contact info@zflite.com.

What stays on the device

The app keeps a few settings on the phone or tablet itself: the name you type on the first screen, the level you have reached, and whether you accepted the opening notice. Removing the app removes those settings.

What is sent to Firebase

When a level is saved, the app writes a record to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). The record is used to show your debrief and to compute the average and the best result on that level.

A saved record can include:

  • the name you entered
  • a device identifier created by the operating system
  • the text on the FMC scratchpad at the moment of the save, if there is any
  • average altitude, landing-gear altitude, average flap altitude, speedbrake time, and fuel remaining

The same Firebase SDK can also send Google basic app diagnostics, such as the app version, device model, and session information. Zflite does not add its own analytics events on top of that.

What we do not collect

The app has no account password, no advertising, and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, or microphone. Contact us opens your own email app. Zflite receives that message only if you send it.

Checking for an update asks Google Play or the Apple App Store whether a newer build exists. That check does not include your name or your level results.

Who receives it

Google hosts the Firebase records and processes them for us. If you install Zflite from Google Play or the App Store, that store also handles the download under its own privacy policy. We do not sell this information and we do not use it to advertise to you.

Level averages and best results are shown inside the game. They are totals for a level, not a public profile of a named player.

How long it is kept

A level record stays until you ask us to delete it, or until we delete it because it is no longer needed to run the debrief. Write to info@zflite.com from the same name you used in the app and say that you want the record deleted. Device settings disappear when you delete the app.

Children

Zflite is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us a name or a level record, email info@zflite.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If the app starts collecting a new kind of information, this page will change and the date at the top will change with it.

Zflite is a training simulation. It is not for real-world navigation.
